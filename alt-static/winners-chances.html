Who Can Win the Porra

One page, one question: which player can still win the game — driven by the real quarter-final bracket

⚽ Quarter-finals are set. We take each player's locked points (group winners + 🎁 Round of 16 extra) and add every point they could still earn, using the real bracket: semis 2 pts each, finalists 4, 3rd/4th teams 3, champion 10, 3rd-place 2, top scorer 5. Every quarter-finalist gets an equal chance to advance — except two teams who meet in the same quarter-final, where only one can reach the semis. The Golden Boot is a coin-flip between Mbappé, Messi & Haaland. Knocked-out teams (Germany, Netherlands, Brazil, Portugal, USA, Colombia…) score nothing.
