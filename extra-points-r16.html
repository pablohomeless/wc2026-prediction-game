Extra Points — Round of 16

Everyone's picks for Brazil vs Norway and Mexico vs England.

� The bonus round in a nutshell

The best and coldest cards of the two Round of 16 bonus games — based only on these results.

Brazil culture clash Norway
🌞
Weather: Rio can hit a sweaty 35°C on the beach, while Oslo is happy with a "warm" 20°C — Norwegians pack a jacket just in case, in July.
🍽️
Food: Sizzling churrasco and feijoada vs. brown cheese (brunost), salmon and the mighty cinnamon bun. Both agree coffee is non-negotiable.
🎉
Celebrations: Carnival's samba, feathers and drums against Norway's midsummer bonfires and 17 May parades in crisp folk costumes.
⚽
Football lore: Five-time champions and the home of "jogo bonito" — yet Norway famously never lost to Brazil in their first four meetings. Underdog fuel!
Sunscreen vs. windbreaker, samba vs. fjords — either way, someone's dancing after this one. 🕺
Mexico culture clash England
🌦️
Weather: Sun-drenched plazas and warm evenings vs. the classic English "four seasons in one afternoon" — bring an umbrella and a taco.
🍽️
Food: Tacos al pastor, mole and spicy salsas meet fish & chips, Sunday roast and a proper cup of tea. Chili heat vs. gravy comfort.
🎉
Celebrations: Day of the Dead marigolds and mariachi vs. bonfire night, bank-holiday BBQs and singing in the stands.
⚽
Football lore: Mexico hosted two World Cups and gave us the Wave; England lifted the trophy in '66 — and yes, they've met in a famous '86 quarter-final story.
Sombrero vs. flat cap, mariachi vs. terrace chants — turn up the volume. 🎺
